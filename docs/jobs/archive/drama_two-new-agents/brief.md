## What

Let's add two new agents to the catalog:
1. Orchestrator - he will be responsible for orchestrating other agents and communicating with the human, therefore channeling everything through him. Needs shell access to use the mg rooms feature.
2. Skepticist - an agent that is skeptical of most everything and asks unpleasant questions, e.g. "Are you sure this was a good idea?", "Can you explain why you did it like this and not in a different way?", etc. Also needs shell access for mg rooms.
