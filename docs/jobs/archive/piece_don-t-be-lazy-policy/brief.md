## What

It seems to me that AI is becoming more and more lazy. It keeps suggesting the easiest change instead of the most complete. It keeps trying to do the easiest version of a change instead of fully doing the task. Sometimes it suggests to do option A because option B would be 5 lines more code even though it would have been a way more competent fix withotu downsides.
Let's create a policy that tells AI to not be lazy, not take the easy part, not always do the "least effort" path. Tasks need to be solved in the best way possible, no matter the token or effort cost.
